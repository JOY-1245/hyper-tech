Lite unit: strobed UV and NIR screening over a conveyor

Design model, not measured hardware. Items pass under the hood, get a UV fluorescence frame then an NIR frame, and suspect items are pushed into the quarantine bin at the ejector.

365 nm UV-A strobeViolet bars inside the hood. Fluorescence is read through a 450 nm long-pass filter on a monochrome global-shutter camera.
850 and 940 nm NIR strobeDark-red bars. The 940/850 ratio maps surface moisture and tissue absorption.
Encoder and timing controllerThe wheel under the belt reports position. The controller schedules the pusher from the encoder count, not from software timing.
Edge compute boxRaspberry Pi 5 with an AI accelerator, mounted on the hood. It scores each region as clean or suspect.
Pusher and quarantine binFlagged items are pushed sideways at the ejector. Leafy greens could use an air jet instead.
